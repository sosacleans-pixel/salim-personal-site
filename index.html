About

From civil engineering to a life built around well-being.

I'm Jorge Salim Heyaime Martorell, and most people call me Salim. I'm based in Utah. My path into fitness and wellness started from a personal desire for better health, longevity and functionality: not just to move well today, but to thrive for years to come. What began as my own journey has grown into a lifelong mission: guiding others to discover what they're truly capable of.

I didn't start as a coach. I'm a civil engineer and project manager (PMP) — BS in Civil Engineering from UNIBE — and I worked in the Dominican Republic before I found the connection between movement, nutrition and mindset. Today I bring fitness, nutrition, yoga and functional training together in a holistic approach built on sustainable habits, resilience and a real connection between mind and body.

“It doesn't get easier; we get stronger.”
