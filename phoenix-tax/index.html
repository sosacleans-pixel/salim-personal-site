Document checklist

What to gather before you upload

Not everything applies to everyone. Bring what you have and we will let you know if anything is missing.

Personal returns

  • W-2 forms from every employer
  • 1099 forms (NEC, MISC, INT, DIV, B, R, G, K, SSA-1099)
  • 1098 mortgage interest statement
  • 1098-T tuition and 1098-E student loan interest
  • 1095-A if you had Marketplace health insurance
  • Last year's tax return
  • Photo ID for you and your spouse
  • Social Security cards or ITIN letters for everyone on the return
  • Childcare provider name, address and tax ID
  • Bank routing and account number for direct deposit

Self-employed and business

  • Income records, invoices and 1099-K forms
  • Business expense receipts or bank statements
  • Mileage log for business driving
  • Home office size and expenses
  • Payroll reports and contractor payments
  • Equipment or vehicle purchases
  • Estimated tax payments made during the year

Starting a company

  • Two or three business name ideas
  • Names and addresses of all owners
  • Business address and a short description of what you do
  • Ownership percentages and who manages the company
  • Your preferred domain name, if you have one

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to meet in person?

No. You can do everything online, from uploading documents to reviewing and approving your return. If you prefer to talk it through, we can set up a call.

How do I send my documents?

Through the client portal. Sign in with your email, follow the checklist and upload photos or files. Please do not send tax documents by regular email.

Is the client portal open now?

The portal is in preview. You can try the demo to see how it works, but please do not upload real documents yet.

Can you help if I already have a business?

Yes. We can help with annual reports, a registered agent, an EIN, bookkeeping cleanup or getting your business online, even if you formed your company somewhere else.

Do you give legal advice?

No. Phoenix Tax is not a law firm. Our business formation services are document preparation and filing help. For legal questions, please talk to a licensed attorney.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your situation and the services you need. Tell us what you are looking for and we will send you a quote before any work begins.
